How it works

Install the app

Get it free on Google Play. The iPhone version is coming soon to the App Store.

Add your playlist

Enter the Xtream Codes login or M3U link you got from your own TV provider.

Start watching

Your live TV, movies and series load in seconds, all in one place.

Please note: IPTV Smarter Pro is a media player only. It does not include, sell or provide any channels, movies, series or subscriptions. You need a playlist or login from your own provider, and you should only add content you have the right to watch.
